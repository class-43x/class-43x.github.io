Getting started with Paper.io 3

Paper.io 3 is listed as a action game. Start by trying the listed inputs (WASD / Arrow Keys / Mouse) and getting comfortable with how they respond.

What to expect

Control a block on a 3D heart, move around and capture territory. Do this by drawing a line and touching back with your own territory, this will fill in the area you drew. You will die if you or another player hits your drawn lines. You can also kill other players by hitting their drawn lines.

Tips for your first session

Use the opening screen to identify the goal and try one input at a time. Watch how the game responds before attempting a longer run.
