Getting started with Block the Pig

Block the Pig is listed as a puzzle game. Start by trying the listed inputs (Click) and getting comfortable with how they respond.

What to expect

In this simple but addicting puzzle game Block the Pig, your goal is to place blocks wisely to form an enclosure around the pig without letting it escape! If the pig touches the edge of the screen, the game's over. You need to think of creative ways to use the blocks already on the screen since you only have a limited amount of blocks to use. As you progress through the game, each round will get harder, but if you place the blocks just right, you can still block the pig! Have fun blocking that poor little creature!

Tips for your first session

Look at what a move opens or closes before making it. If you get stuck, return to the first decision that narrowed your options.
