Getting started with 2048

2048 is listed as a number game. Start by trying the listed inputs (Arrow / WASD Keys) and getting comfortable with how they respond.

What to expect

2048 is a very popular and classic puzzle game. The goal is to merge the tiles with the same numbers until they merge all the way to 2048. Use arrow keys to move all the tiles. If no tiles can be moved anymore, you fail.

Tips for your first session

Look at what a move opens or closes before making it. If you get stuck, return to the first decision that narrowed your options.
