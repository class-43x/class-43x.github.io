Getting started with Idle Mining Empire

Idle Mining Empire is listed as a idle game. Start by trying the listed inputs (Mouse) and getting comfortable with how they respond.

What to expect

Idle Mining Empire is a very fun idle managing mining game! Your goal is to hire workers, buy and upgrade shafts, and earn the most money possible! You can click on the miner or hire a manager to mine. Click or hire another manager for the elevator to transport money. Finally, hire one more manager to get the money. The more shafts and upgrades you have, the more money you can earn while you're away. You can even prestige and get a huge earning bonus later in the game! Do your best and become the biggest mining empire!

Tips for your first session

Compare what each available purchase changes before spending. Use early progress to see which choices keep paying off over time.
